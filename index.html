A LETTER FOR SHIVANI

Dear Shivani, meri favourite troublemaker.

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki kitni ajeeb si baat hai — hum dono bas ek GC mein mile the. Na koi plan tha, na idea ki ek random online mulaqat itni saari memories ka hissa ban jayegi.

Phir baatein hui, attachment hui, aur ek waqt aisa bhi aaya jab friendship se zyada feelings hone lagi. Baad mein humne us feeling ko ek naam dene ke bajaye apni dosti ko choose kiya. Aur aaj tum meri best friend ho — apne alag hi, thode crazy, thode complicated tareeke se.

Humari story koi perfect movie nahi hai. Isme fights hain, gussa hai, block-unblock hai, ego hai, aur phir kabhi na kabhi baat karne ki wajah bhi. Shayad isi liye yeh story real lagti hai. Hum hamesha easy nahi rahe, lekin tum mere liye kabhi bas “GC ki ek ladki” nahi rahi.

Bas tumhe ek baat yaad dilani thi, Shivani. ♡
